Passel is a password manager built on pass, the standard Unix password store. Your passwords stay in a plain pass vault: one OpenPGP-encrypted file per entry, kept where you choose. No account, no Passel server, no lock-in: Passel on your phone and computer, the pass command line and every other pass app read and write the same vault.

ENCRYPTED WITH YOUR OWN KEY
• Every entry is encrypted with your OpenPGP key, on your phone, before it is stored or synced.
• Create a key in the setup wizard, or import the one you already use with pass on a computer.
• Save a backup of a new key right away, still protected by its passphrase.

SYNCED YOUR WAY
• Google Drive: keep your vault in your own Drive.
• Git: any private repository (GitHub, GitLab, Gitea, your own server), over HTTPS.
• Changes made on the phone and on the computer merge. When the same entry changed on both sides, nothing is lost.
• Works offline: changes wait on the phone until the next sync.

ORGANIZED
• Several vaults: personal, work, shared with family or a team. Switch between them from the title bar.
• Entries grouped by folder, with search.
• Fields of your own next to the password: username, URL, notes, recovery codes…
• One-time password (TOTP) codes with a countdown.
• A password generator.

CAREFUL WITH SECRETS
• Unlock with your fingerprint or face, instead of typing your key's passphrase.
• Locks by itself after 2 minutes in the background.
• Copied passwords are cleared from the clipboard, secrets are hidden from the keyboard, and the app blocks screenshots.
• No ads, no tracking, no analytics.

ALSO ON YOUR COMPUTER
Passel is also a desktop app for Linux and Windows, on the same vaults.

In English, French, German, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese.
